Midnight Burner VR – Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Midnight Burner VR does not collect, sell or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What stays on your device

Everything you make is stored only on your headset or computer, in the browser storage the app uses:

  • walls you paint and save, and their thumbnails
  • photos and time-lapse videos you take in the game
  • music files you choose to add
  • settings such as your projector word and preferences

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes it.

Files loaded from other services

To run, the app downloads its 3D engine from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, those services receive your device's IP address and basic browser details when the files are requested. After the first launch the app keeps copies on your device and works offline. See the Cloudflare and Google privacy policies.

Permissions

The app uses VR head and controller tracking to play. It does not use your camera, microphone, location or contacts. You pick any music files yourself; the app reads only the files you choose.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
