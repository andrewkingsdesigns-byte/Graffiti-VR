How to play

In VR

  • Left stick walks where you look · flick the right stick to turn (teleport is an option on the tablet)
  • Right trigger sprays · get close: spray only reaches about 60 cm
  • Shake your hand to build pressure: the can sputters as pressure drops and runs out of paint. Pick the colour again for a fresh can
  • If the cap clogs, press B to flip the can (or shake it hard)
  • A undo · X next track
  • Y brings out the tablet in front of you (press again to put it away). Reach for it and squeeze the left grip to pick it up; let go and it stays floating where you left it
  • Point at the tablet's screen with your right hand and pull the trigger to choose
  • Tablet → Tools: spray can, roller (big areas), stencils, Save wall, Camera, Time-lapse
  • Camera: aim the tablet and pull the left trigger to shoot
  • Stencil: hold left grip, press it flat on the wall, spray, let go to peel it off
  • Tablet → Project: faint guide lines on the wall: your word, your own sketch, and more
  • Marker: touch the wall with the tip, or hold the trigger with the tip close to the wall, and draw
  • Stickers: Tablet → Tools → Stickers. Pick one, point at the wall within arm's reach and pull the trigger to slap it on (in the browser: click the wall)
  • Stencils and projections: more hip-hop stencils (boombox, mic, cassette, kicks, decks, b-boy and more) and new templates: wildstyle and handstyle versions of your word, boombox, b-boy, mic & crown, turntables, fill-ins and a backdrop
  • Point at the boombox and pull the trigger to skip tracks

In the browser

  • Hold left mouse on the wall to spray or roll (Shift feathers) · scroll for can distance
  • Hold right mouse for colours (scroll for sets) · hold Q or press 1–9 for caps
  • Hold R to shake · F flip (clears a clogged cap)
  • E sticks a stencil at the cursor, E again peels it
  • W A S D walk · drag off the wall to look
  • Ctrl Z undo · Ctrl S save · P photo · T time-lapse · N next track

Your work

  • Walls autosave every minute and whenever you leave; Continue on this screen picks up where you left off
  • Gallery holds your photos and time-lapse videos
  • Import sketch: a photo of a drawing becomes projector guide lines (do this here, before entering VR)
  • My music: add your own songs to the mix
  • Browser: click the boombox to skip tracks
